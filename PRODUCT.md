# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Home cooks and individuals managing their household food supply who want to stay healthy without friction. They use this in a private, home environment (primarily on a mobile device in the kitchen) to manage inventory, find inspiration, and track nutrition.

## Product Purpose
TUYEN (Smart Fridge Chef) exists to solve food waste (forgetting food until it rots), meal decision fatigue ("what should I cook?"), and the friction of traditional calorie tracking.
A successful session:
1. **Instant Visibility:** The user opens the app on their phone and instantly sees all current fridge items in a single view.
2. **Zero-Friction Scan:** Taps a large, circular floating action button (FAB) at the bottom center to photo-scan new groceries, allowing the AI to automatically analyze and update inventory.
3. **Rapid AI Chat:** Slides up the bottom sheet to chat with SmartFood AI, which recommends suitable recipes using ingredients close to their expiration dates.

## Brand Personality
Premium, distinct, frictionless, and home-friendly (Anti-AI Slop). The tone is highly user-friendly, exceptionally simple, and lightning-fast. It feels like a high-end personal kitchen assistant rather than a generic utility.

## Anti-references
Boring, cookie-cutter dashboard templates. Generic utility apps that require excessive clicks or complex menus.

## Design Principles
1. **Instant Clarity:** Inventory is visible the moment the app opens. No digging required.
2. **Zero Friction:** Inputs (scanning, logging) require minimal interaction and a single floating action button (FAB).
3. **High-Speed Utility:** AI and suggestions slide up instantly in a bottom sheet when needed.
4. **Mobile First & Premium (Tooyen Core):** The experience is strictly optimized for mobile screens, feeling native, fast, and elegantly distinct.
5. **Tooyen Core Visual Style:**
   - **Premium & Accessible (Light Theme):** Use bright tones (Tinted Neutrals) mixed with light blue accents. Avoid dark modes or pure black/dark grey.
   - **Soft-Tactile:** Cards and chat bubbles must have rounded corners (`rounded-2xl` or pill-shape), a solid `2px` white border, and soft light blue drop shadows to feel clean, tactile, and friendly.
   - **Fluid Layout:** Maintain plenty of whitespace and breathable layouts. Avoid rigid tables or boxy grids to make the app feel warm and home-friendly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Designed for fast, one-handed mobile use in a kitchen environment (e.g., cooking, carrying groceries). High contrast for readability at a glance, with clear, unambiguous iconography.
